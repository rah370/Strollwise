StrollWise Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 August 2026

StrollWise is a crowd-sourced travel intelligence app for Metro Cebu. This policy describes what we collect, how we use it, and who can see it. It is written for a research / thesis demonstration as well as a public hosted demo.

What we collect

  • Account details: email, display name, password hash, traveler type, and origin city/country.
  • Location when you submit a tag: approximate GPS used to assign an H3 cell. We request location only while the app is in use.
  • Tag content: category, tags, optional notes, and optional photos.
  • Technical logs: coarse IP for rate limiting and error reports (if Sentry is enabled).

How location is shown

Public APIs expose aggregated zones (traveler mix and place type over H3 cells). Individual trajectories and exact GPS points are not published on Explore. Raw reports, including coordinates and user ids, are visible to operators with admin accounts for moderation and research validation only.

Research use

If you accept research consent at registration, your tags may be converted into aggregated zone signals used in academic research about crowd-sourced place intelligence. You can refuse consent by not creating an account.

Retention

Account and report data is kept while the hosted demo or research project is active. Operators may delete abusive accounts. Backups (for example Supabase point-in-time recovery) may retain copies for a limited window after deletion.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Processors may include the database host (Postgres/PostGIS), object storage for photos, error monitoring, and map geocoding (Mapbox) when those services are configured.

Your choices

You can stop using the app at any time. To request access or deletion of your account, contact the operator listed on the hosted site. Public zone maps cannot be fully un-aggregated once reports have been merged into cells.
